Features

Clockwork offers a range of features to help you manage your time effectively!

1.Lifecycle-Aware Scheduling

Clockwork understands that your life stages have unique demands and rhythms. It adapts your schedule based on your current situation, ensuring that your time is allocated where it matters most.

2.Energy Curve Intelligence

Clockwork tracks your energy levels throughout the day and uses this data to optimize task allocation. High-energy periods are reserved for demanding tasks, while lower-energy times are used for less intensive activities.

3.Mood-Aware Rescheduling

Clockwork takes into account your emotional state when rescheduling tasks. If you're feeling overwhelmed or stressed, it can adjust your schedule to include more rest or lower-priority activities, ensuring a better work-life balance.

4.Weekly Adaptation Loop

Clockwork treats your schedule as a living document, not a fixed plan. At the close of each week, it analyses completion patterns, identifies what consistently slips, and restructures the following week to address those gaps proactively. Over time, the system grows more accurate, more realistic, and more aligned with how you actually work.

5.Time Debt Tracker

Clockwork tracks your time debt — the cumulative effect of consistently overcommitting or underestimating tasks. This visibility helps you make informed decisions about future commitments and prioritize recovery time.

6.Ideal Day Simulator

Clockwork gives you the ability to design your day before you live it. Using plain language, you can model any change to your routine — an earlier start, a new habit, a shifted work block — and instantly see how it reshapes the rest of your day. Decisions that once required weeks of trial and error can now be evaluated, refined, and confirmed in seconds.

How It Works

Clockwork uses advanced algorithms to analyze your habits, preferences, and schedule. It then provides personalized recommendations to help you optimize your time and achieve your goals.

STEP 1 - Data Collection

Clockwork collects data on your daily activities, energy levels, and mood through a combination of manual input and passive tracking. This data forms the foundation for its personalized scheduling recommendations.

STEP 2 - Your Schedule, Built by AI

Clockwork takes your answers and generates a fully structured daily schedule — not a blank calendar waiting to be filled, but a realistic, personalised plan ready to use from day one. Tasks are slotted to the right hours, recovery time is built in, and your constraints are respected from the start.

STEP 3 - Continuous Optimization

Clockwork continuously learns from your interactions and feedback. As you complete tasks, adjust your schedule, or provide input on your energy and mood, Clockwork refines its recommendations to better suit your evolving needs.
